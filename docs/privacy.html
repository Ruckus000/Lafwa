Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

🔒 Lafwa is designed with your privacy as a priority. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Our Privacy Commitment

Lafwa ("the App") is built to work completely offline. This means:

  • We never see what Bible passages you read
  • We never see what hymns you search for
  • We never see your bookmarks, highlights, or favorites
  • We never track your usage patterns
  • We never share any data with third parties

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Bookmarks — Bible verses you bookmark for quick access
  • Highlights — Verses you highlight with colors
  • Favorites — Hymns you mark as favorites
  • Reading History — Recently viewed chapters and hymns
  • Settings — Your preferences like font size, theme, and language
  • Notes — Any personal notes you create

This data never leaves your device. We cannot access it, and it is not backed up to any cloud service. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this information will be permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services

Lafwa does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting tools that transmit data

Network Usage

After the initial download from the App Store or Google Play, Lafwa does not require any internet connection to function. The App may optionally check for updates through the app stores, but this is handled by iOS/Android system services, not by us.

Children's Privacy

Lafwa does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages and contains only Bible scripture and traditional hymns.

Data Portability

Currently, your bookmarks, highlights, and other data cannot be exported from the App. This is a local-only feature by design. We may add export functionality in future versions.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated through an app update.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Lafwa handles your data, please contact us:

  • Email: support@lafwa.app
  • GitHub: Open an issue
